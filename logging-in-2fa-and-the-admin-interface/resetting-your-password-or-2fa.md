# Resetting your password or 2FA

### Resetting your password

If you've forgotten your password, go to **/user/password** and submit a password reset by entering your email address and selecting the **Submit** button.

{% hint style="info" %}
If you're currently in training and don't have access to your email address, please ask your trainer to reset your password manually.
{% endhint %}

### Resetting your 2FA

The [GovCMS Knowledge Base](https://www.govcms.support/support/solutions/articles/51000386377) contains a number of articles on troubleshooting 2FA issues, including resetting your 2FA if you lose access to your device.

{% hint style="info" %}
If you're currently in training you shouldn't need your 2FA reset. However if you're having trouble with your 2FA, please ask your trainer.
{% endhint %}
