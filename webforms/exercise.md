# Exercise

### Exercise 1

Create a simple webform with a **Name** field and **Email address** field and have it send an email to the email address entered in the form.

### Exercise 2

Add some additional interesting field types. Include a **Select** field that you can use to hide and show the fields based on conditional logic.

### Exercise 3

Using the **Test** function, add a bunch of submissions to your form and then download them.
