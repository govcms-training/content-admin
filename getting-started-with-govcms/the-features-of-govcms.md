# The features of GovCMS

GovCMS includes many features tailored for Australian government websites, including:

* **Publishing workflows** so content can move from ‘Draft’ to ‘Needs review’ to ‘Published’ (live)
* **Website analytics** to give you access to insights about your users
* **Webforms** to collect and report information/data from your users
* **Compliance** with Australian Government website requirements, metadata standards and WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility
* The **ability to easily edit content** without any HTML skills
* **Responsive display** so GovCMS websites can be viewed (and look good) on phones and tablets
* **Standard content types** for news, events, FoI, standard pages and blogs
* **Metadata** support for search engine optimisation (SEO)
* The ability to **manage media**, such as photos and videos
* **Forms** to collect data from the public
* **Custom lists** such as a compiled list of articles, media releases, publications or other types of content
* **Unique designs** with no limit on your website's theme (while GovCMS is a whole-of-government digital platform, it still caters for many different and visually attractive designs and different navigation structures — see a list of websites using GovCMS on the [GovCMS Gallery](https://www.govcms.gov.au/gallery)
* A robust **menu system** allowing for rich and specific information architecture (IA).
