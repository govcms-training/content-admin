# What this manual does not cover

The following topics won't be covered in the training or this training manual. They're either not available or may be covered in the Advanced Content Management training:

* Onboarding to GovCMS
* User stories or persona mapping
* Website audit and reviews
* Development tasks such as:
  * Creating or modifying content types or fields
  * Designing menu architecture or major navigational restructuring
  * Creating views or modifying listing pages
  * Configuring pathauto or advanced redirects
  * Configuring complex webforms or integrations
  * Theming, layout configuration or component creation
  * Any coding.
